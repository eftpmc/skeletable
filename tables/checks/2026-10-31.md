## What was wrong

- **Railway, free offer.** We wrote "30-day free trial with $5 credits, then $1 per month", reading a charge into wording that means free credit. Railway's docs: "the free trial reverts to the free plan, which provides $1 of free credit per month".
- **Fly.io, free offer.** We used the slogan "Start for free, pay as you grow". The words are on the page, but they don't say what you get for free. The cell is now missing.

Before this check, a self-review also caught a Netlify cell claiming more than its quote. It isn't in the 96%, which scores the tables as they stood going into the check. Counting it, 48 of 51 (94%) were right as first written.

All three were descriptive text. Every number was right.

## How far to trust this

- Fifty-one cells across two tables is an early read, not proof of 95%.
- The blind reader is also an AI, so a mistake both models make the same way wouldn't show. No person has checked this check.
- Web fetching can't reach archive copies, and read Render's pricing page without its prices. So MSI's 10 cells, Lenovo's weight and Render's 3 cells were compared against summaries of the owners' own pages from search, a weaker check than a direct blind read.
- The check covers whether values are correct, not whether either table is complete.
